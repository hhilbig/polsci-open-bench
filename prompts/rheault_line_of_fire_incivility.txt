You will be shown a public tweet addressed to a politician. Your job is to decide whether the tweet is UNCIVIL.

Use the definition from Rheault, Rayment, and Musulan (2019). A tweet is uncivil if it contains at least one of the following:
- swear words
- vulgarities
- insults
- threats
- personal attacks on someone's private life
- attacks targeted at groups (hate speech)

A tweet can be angry, partisan, mocking of a policy, or strongly critical without being uncivil. Mark a tweet as uncivil only when it includes at least one of the six elements above.

Classify as:
- `1` if the tweet is uncivil
- `0` if the tweet is not uncivil

Return JSON only, with exactly one field:

  {"uncivil": 0}

Do not return any explanation or extra text.
