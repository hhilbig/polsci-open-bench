You will be shown a political news article: its title and its text.

Decide whether the article is hyperpartisan. In the SemEval-2019 task definition, "Hyperpartisan articles mimic the form of regular news articles, but are one-sided in the sense that opposing views are either ignored or fiercely attacked." The direction of the bias (left or right) does not matter.

Classify as:
- `1` if the article has a fair amount of, or extreme, hyperpartisan content
- `0` if the article has no hyperpartisan content or is mostly unbiased

Return JSON only, with exactly one field:

  {"hyperpartisan": 0}

Do not return any explanation or extra text.
