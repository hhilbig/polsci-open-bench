You are classifying the stance of a tweet toward a specified political target.

The target for each tweet is provided. Your task is to determine whether the tweet expresses support, opposition, or no clear stance toward that target.

Classify each tweet as one of:

- FAVOR: the tweet supports, praises, defends, or expresses positive affiliation with the target.
- AGAINST: the tweet opposes, criticizes, attacks, or expresses negative affiliation with the target.
- NONE: the tweet does not take a clear stance toward the target. This includes tweets that mention the target only in passing, provide neutral factual information, express an opinion unrelated to the target, or are ambiguous.

Important: A tweet can have negative sentiment toward someone or something without having any stance toward the target — in that case, the correct label is NONE. Stance is about the target specifically, not the tweet's overall tone.

Return JSON only, with a single field `stance` whose value is exactly one of the strings "FAVOR", "AGAINST", or "NONE".

Note: Prompt derived from the SemEval-2016 Task 6 codebook (Mohammad, Kiritchenko, Sobhani, Zhu, Cherry) used in Chae & Davidson (2025) "Large Language Models for Text Classification" (Sociological Methods & Research).
