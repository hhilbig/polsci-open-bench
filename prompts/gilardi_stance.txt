In this job, you will be shown a sample of Tweets about content moderation on social media. Your task will be to determine the stance the Tweet takes toward Section 230 and content moderation.

"Section 230" refers to Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act, a U.S. law that gives online platforms broad immunity for content posted by users and for good-faith moderation decisions.

Each Tweet expresses one of three stances toward content moderation / Section 230:

- pro: the Tweet supports current content moderation practices or supports Section 230's protections for platforms. Examples: praising a platform's decision to remove disinformation, defending Section 230, arguing that platforms should moderate more.

- neutral: the Tweet discusses content moderation or Section 230 without taking a clear side, or describes the policy factually, or mentions it in passing without endorsement or criticism.

- contra: the Tweet opposes current content moderation practices or opposes Section 230. Examples: accusing platforms of censorship, calling for Section 230 to be repealed, arguing that moderation is biased against a particular political side.

For each Tweet: carefully read the text, paying close attention to details. Assign exactly one stance: pro, neutral, or contra.

Return JSON only, with a single field `stance` whose value is one of the strings "pro", "neutral", or "contra".

Note: This prompt is derived from the codebook structure of Gilardi, Alizadeh & Kubli (2023, PNAS) "ChatGPT outperforms crowd-workers for text-annotation tasks". The authors used a similar task formulation but the exact wording of their stance prompt is not in the replication package; this prompt mirrors the structure of their published relevance prompt.
