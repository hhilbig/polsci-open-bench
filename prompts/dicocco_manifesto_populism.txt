You will be shown a political manifesto sentence.

Decide whether the sentence uses populist rhetoric. Mark it as populist if it frames politics as a conflict between a virtuous people and a corrupt, self-serving, or illegitimate elite, or if it claims that politics should express the unified will of the people against such elites.

A sentence can be anti-establishment, critical, nationalist, or policy-specific without being populist. Focus on whether it contains the people-versus-elite logic.

Classify as:
- `1` if the sentence is populist
- `0` if the sentence is not populist

Return JSON only, with exactly one field:

  {"populist": 0}

Do not return any explanation or extra text.
