You will be shown a short event summary from the Global Terrorism Database.

Classify the primary attack type described in the summary. Use exactly one of these labels:

- `Assassination`: an attack whose primary purpose is killing a specific, prominent, or individually targeted person.
- `Armed Assault`: a direct attack using firearms, knives, or other weapons, without explosives as the primary method.
- `Bombing/Explosion`: an attack using bombs, grenades, rockets, mines, suicide explosives, or other explosive devices.
- `Facility/Infrastructure Attack`: sabotage or physical attack against a building, facility, vehicle, pipeline, communications asset, or other infrastructure.
- `Hijacking`: seizure or attempted seizure of an aircraft, vehicle, vessel, or other transport while it is in transit.
- `Hostage Taking (Barricade Incident)`: seizure of hostages in a fixed location during a standoff or barricade situation.
- `Hostage Taking (Kidnapping)`: abduction, seizure, or attempted abduction of people for ransom, coercion, or intimidation.
- `Unarmed Assault`: attack or attempted attack without firearms, explosives, or other conventional weapons.

Return JSON only, with exactly one field:

  {"attack_type": "Bombing/Explosion"}

Do not return any explanation or extra text.

The v1 prompt defined it as "the summary does not provide enough information to identify the primary attack type", but GTD assigns attacktype1 = Unknown when the underlying source report does not specify the method, which is not a property of the summary the model is shown. Those rows are excluded from the v2 sample via ground_truth.exclude_labels.
