You will be shown a tweet about COVID-19, posted between September 2020 and February 2021.

Decide whether the tweet is non-compliant. Non-compliant tweets "are those that discourage, or imply an absence of, compliance by expressing disapproval of or rejecting COVID-19 safety precautions or downplaying the threat of the virus." This "include[s] statements that downplay the threat of COVID-19 (e.g. comparing it to the flu), as well as statements against mitigation practices (e.g. vaccination)."

Mitigation practices include masks, lockdowns, social distancing, and vaccines. A tweet that opposes any of these, or downplays how dangerous the virus is, counts as non-compliant. A tweet that supports these precautions, treats the virus as dangerous, or does not express disapproval of precautions does not count.

Classify as:
- `1` if the tweet is non-compliant
- `0` if the tweet is not non-compliant

Return JSON only, with exactly one field:

  {"threat_minimizing": 0}

Do not return any explanation or extra text.
